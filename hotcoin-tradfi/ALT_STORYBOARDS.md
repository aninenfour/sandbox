# Hotcoin TradFi: motion graphics alternative, 3 storyboard variants

**Shared by all three:**
- **Format:** 4:5 (1080x1350) at 60 fps and 120 BPM, about 18 s (36 beats). No UI recording.
- **Brand:** ink, green, paper and red; Archivo Black, IBM Plex Mono and Instrument Serif italic.
- **End card:** the Hotcoin logo (keyed out of the brand recording, never redrawn) and the URL.
- **Cursor:** drives each change.
- **Rules:** one message on screen at a time, and no crossfades.
- **Messages:**
  1. Tesla, NVIDIA, Apple, gold and ETFs with USDT.
  2. Spot and Futures in one account, with no moving funds and no fiat account.
  3. From 10 USDT on selected products.
  4. End card.

Hero stills: `stills/alt-A-orbit.png`, `stills/alt-B-halftone.png`, `stills/alt-C-horizon.png`.

## A. Orbit
**Reference:** *X: timeline, ticker, trade* (whatships.com/videos/x-ticker). A bright paper stage, orbit rings, network lines and a single thin price line.

| Beats | Time | What moves | Text |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1-2 | 0-1 s | The cursor clicks on paper. A green dot pops and grows into the USDT coin, and rings ripple out as hard strokes | none |
| 3-10 | 1-5 s | Ticker chips (AAPL, NVDA, TSLA, GOLD, ETFs, SILVER) snap onto the orbits one per beat. The cursor drags NVDA inward and a green line locks it to the coin. The whole system turns slowly | **Trade the world with USDT** |
| 11-14 | 5-7 s | Two rings labeled SPOT and FUTURES counter-rotate, then collapse into one ring | **Spot and Futures.** then **One account.** |
| 15-18 | 7-9 s | The cursor tries to drag a "transfer" line between the rings. It snaps back, because there is nowhere to transfer to | **No moving funds.** |
| 19-20 | 9-10 s | A dashed ring labeled FIAT dissolves stroke by stroke | **No fiat account.** |
| 21-28 | 10-14 s | The price line rises through the orbits and lifts the coin, which flips to show "10" | **From 10 USDT** + footnote |
| 29-36 | 14-18 s | The rings contract into the coin, which becomes a dot. The cursor clicks and ink floods the frame for the end card | Logo + URL |

**Feels like:** calm, product-launch, Apple-bright. **Lowest risk.**

## B. Halftone
**Reference:** *Cube Motion* (whatships.com/videos/cube-motion). Everything is one dot grid that re-forms into each message, with code-style labels.

| Beats | Time | What moves | Text |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1-2 | 0-1 s | Faint dot grid on ink. The cursor clicks, and one dot grows and turns green | `trade()` |
| 3-10 | 1-5 s | The grid dots swarm into **AAPL → NVDA → TSLA → GOLD → ETFs** in giant dot type, re-forming on every beat. A dot candlestick chart ticks below | **with USDT** (green pill) |
| 11-14 | 5-7 s | The dots split into two dot-letter words, SPOT and FUTURES, then merge into one dot circle | `spot() + futures() = one account` |
| 15-20 | 7-10 s | The cursor drags a dot "transfer" arrow, which scatters back into the grid. The dot word FIAT drains row by row | **No moving funds.** **No fiat account.** |
| 21-28 | 10-14 s | The grid condenses into a huge dot-matrix **10**, and every dot inside it flips to green | **From 10 USDT** + footnote |
| 29-36 | 14-18 s | The dots fall into one green dot. The cursor clicks and ink floods the frame for the end card | Logo + URL |

**Feels like:** engineered and graphic, very "motion designer". **The most original look for an exchange.**

## C. Horizon
**Reference:** *Claude Opus 5.5* (whatships.com/videos/claude-opus-5-5). Macro film textures cut by one curved horizon, with a single serif phrase on the edge. The arc stays still and the texture match-cuts on every beat.

| Beats | Time | What moves (always the same arc) | Text on the horizon |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1-2 | 0-1 s | Black, then a hairline arc draws itself. The cursor clicks on it | none |
| 3-4 | 1-2 s | **Car hood at night**, with bokeh on the paint | *Trade Tesla* |
| 5-6 | 2-3 s | **Macro dollar engraving** | *NVIDIA* |
| 7-8 | 3-4 s | **Gold texture** | *Gold* |
| 9-10 | 4-5 s | **Coins in black and white** | *ETFs* |
| 11-12 | 5-6 s | The arc tilts and turns green | *with USDT.* |
| 13-20 | 6-10 s | **Car roof curve** reflection. The arc splits into two and rejoins | *Spot and Futures.* *One account.* *Nothing to move.* |
| 21-28 | 10-14 s | The horizon becomes the rim of a giant green coin rising | *From 10 USDT* |
| 29-36 | 14-18 s | The coin fills the frame, and its rim becomes the end card's underline | Logo + URL |

**Feels like:** a cinematic brand film; the automobile shots you liked lead it. **The most emotional, but the least literal about the product.**
