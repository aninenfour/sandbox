Logo and art

The mark

A red bubble with a price line inside it: a slow climb, the mania, a peak that breaks through the top and pops, then the crash. That's every episode in one shape. It uses the same red, ink and Anton type as the videos, so the channel page, thumbnails and videos look like one show.

The art is code, so changing a colour or the tagline is a one-line edit and a re-render (youtube/channel/brand.html and render.sh in the repo).

How to make it

Set up the channel

About 30 minutes, in this order. Do it from the company Google account so the channel never depends on one person's login. That avoids the Publer problem you had with the current channel.

  1. Create the channel as a Brand Account

    Signed in as the company account: youtube.com › profile picture › Create a channel. Enter "Bubble Files" as the name. Using a custom name makes it a Brand Account that more than one person can manage.

    youtube.com › Create a channel

  2. Claim the handle

    Set the handle to @bubblefiles straight away.

    Studio › Customization › Basic info › Handle

  3. Verify with a phone number

    This unlocks custom thumbnails and videos longer than 15 minutes. Without it, you can't upload the Ep01 thumbnail.

    youtube.com/verify

  4. Add the team

    Invite the people who upload as Managers or Editors, rather than sharing a password.

    Studio › Settings › Permissions

  5. Upload the art

    Profile picture: profile-800.jpg. Banner: banner-2560x1440.jpg. Watermark: watermark-150.png, set to "Entire video".

    Studio › Customization › Branding

  6. Paste the channel text

    The description and links from above. Then the keywords, set the country, and set the audience to "No, this channel is not made for kids".

    Studio › Customization › Basic info · Settings › Channel

  7. Set upload defaults

    Paste the default description. Category: Education. Language: English. Visibility: Private, so nothing goes live by accident.

    Studio › Settings › Upload defaults

  8. Create the three playlists

    Ep01 goes into Squeezes & Schemes.

    Studio › Content › Playlists

  9. Upload Episode 01 with the pack below

    Then set the channel layout: Ep01 as the featured video for returning subscribers, and the Shorts shelf underneath.

    Studio › Customization › Layout

  10. Connect the channel to Publer

    Because the channel sits under the company account, it shows up when you connect YouTube in Publer. Use Publer to schedule the Shorts across YouTube, TikTok and Instagram.

    Publer › Add account › YouTube

Virality

How it gets views

A new channel has no subscribers to push to, so YouTube tests each video on small groups of strangers and widens the test when they click and keep watching. Two numbers decide everything: how many people click (CTR) and how long they stay. The plan is built around those two.

Packaging before the script

Write the title and draw the thumbnail before writing the episode. If the idea doesn't fit in six words and one image, it won't get clicked. Use a face, one object and one number, and keep the thumbnail to five words or fewer.

The first 30 seconds

Open at the most dramatic moment, then promise the rest. Ep01 opens on grandma's forks being melted at $50 an ounce. Most viewers decide in the first half minute.

Three Shorts per episode

Cut three vertical Shorts from every episode and attach each to the full video with Studio's "Related video" link. Post them over the week after the episode. Shorts reach people who don't know the channel yet.

The first hour

Pin the comment, reply to every early comment, and share in the team's own channels. Don't buy views or subscribers: viewers who don't watch tell YouTube the video is weak.

Read it at 48 hours

Check CTR and average view duration in Analytics. If CTR is low but people who click stay, change the thumbnail; the story is fine. If people leave early, check where in the retention graph they drop and fix that in the next script.

Same look every time

Use the same thumbnail style, the same red and the same series title card, so someone who liked one episode recognises the next one in their feed.

Rules

Stay safe

  • Tick "Includes paid promotion" on every video with the Hotcoin read, and keep the sponsor line in the description. Don't hide that Hotcoin is behind the channel: being found out later costs more trust than any saving.
  • Keep "Futures trading involves risk" in the ad. Some countries (the UK among them) restrict promoting crypto and crypto futures, so check with legal before running paid promotion of these videos.
  • Ep01 uses news photos of the Hunts under fair use and archive films marked public domain. Two films have unclear licence records (the 1958 Hostess outtakes and The Southwestern States); check them before any paid boost. The full list is in FOOTAGE_CREDITS.md.
  • Never use a real person's voice or make it look like someone said something they didn't. That would need YouTube's AI disclosure, and it's the fastest way to lose trust.
