Hotcoin YouTube Reset

The channel needs fewer uploads, one clear reason to watch, and two long series people can put on while they eat.

Scope: channel audit, long-form series, thumbnails, postingGoal: views, not revenue
The short version
  1. The channel is flooded with clips nobody chose to watch. 14 TOKEN2049 clips went up in 6 days and earned 28 views between them. They now cover the top of the Videos tab, so they are the first thing a visitor sees.
  2. Hotcoin 101 is in a format YouTube has nowhere to show. The episodes are horizontal and about 60 seconds long. That is too short for the long-form feed, and horizontal video can't go into the Shorts feed.
  3. Educational and story content beats brand content about 3 to 1. The older educational Shorts have a median of 87 views. The newest 50 brand and campaign Shorts have a median of 28.
  4. The fix: launch two long-form series, Bubble Files (money history) and Price Tag (the economics of everyday things), plus a Sunday 101 compilation. The Tulip Mania 101 already shows the style works.
  5. Posting: keep Publer. Connect the YouTube channel to the Hotcoin workspace (it isn't connected yet), and Claude can schedule every upload with its title, description, tags and custom thumbnail.

3. The long-form series

Brief: broad, recognisable, something people put on while eating. That is lean-back viewing, often on a TV, so the episodes are 10–14 minute stories with large on-screen type, chapters, and a narrator who does most of the work. Both series sit next to what Hotcoin sells (crypto, gold, US stocks) without being about Hotcoin. Each episode ends with a 20-second lesson in the 101 style, then a tease for the next episode.

Series A · Bubble Files

Famous manias, crashes, heists and frauds, told as stories. This genre already does well on YouTube (Magnates Media, Patrick Boyle, Bloomberg Originals). The Tulip Mania 101 is a ready pilot, and it already uses the paper "case file" look, so the two series feel related. Every story carries a trading lesson (leverage, position sizing, custody), so the brand fit comes from the story itself.

Series C · Hotcoin 101, Sunday edition

Five 101 episodes stitched into one 8–10 minute video, for example "Crypto in 10 Minutes: Blockchains, Exchanges, Candles and Slang", with chapters. It costs almost nothing to make, gives the existing 101s a long-form home, and gets search traffic for beginner questions.

4. Thumbnails

Most people decide whether to click from a thumbnail about 168 pixels wide on a phone. The current 101 thumbnails look great at full size, but at that width they're small serif text on beige and can't be read. The new rule: one object, at most four words, high contrast, and the series badge in the same place every time.

6. Posting without the manual work

Recommendation: Publer. Your Hotcoin workspace already has Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn and X connected. YouTube is not connected yet. Once it is, Claude can do the whole upload from a session:

  • Long videos and Shorts, uploaded from the rendered file
  • Title, description (with chapters), tags, category and privacy
  • Custom thumbnail
  • A scheduled publish time, or a draft for you to approve first

Two gaps. Publer's API docs don't cover playlists or the made for kids flag. Set "Not made for kids" once in YouTube Studio → Settings → Upload defaults. Adding each episode to its playlist is a 10-second manual step, or Claude can do it with Claude in Chrome if you use it.

Why not upload straight through the YouTube API? It would cover playlists too, but Google locks every upload from a new, unaudited API project to Private until the project passes a compliance audit. That paperwork isn't worth it while Publer already works.

To connect YouTube in Publer

  1. In Publer, open the Hotcoin workspace → Add account → YouTube.
  2. Sign in with the Google account that owns or manages @hotcoinglobal and choose the Hotcoin channel.
  3. Tell Claude when it's done. Claude will check the connection and schedule a test upload as Private.
