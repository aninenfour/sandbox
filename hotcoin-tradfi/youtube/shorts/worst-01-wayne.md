# Worst Money Decisions 01 · The third Apple founder

**Format:** YouTube Short, 9:16, about 60 seconds. Flat 2D animation, burned-in word captions, a stakes meter in the corner, recreated documents as proof. Ends on a STAY / SELL choice and loops back to the first line.
**Voice:** Kokoro bm_fable (same narrator as Bubble Files), a touch faster than the long video.

One sentence per line. Blank lines are short pauses.

## Script

In 1976, this man owned ten percent of Apple.
Less than two weeks later, he sold it.
For eight hundred dollars.

His name is Ronald Wayne.
He was forty-one. Steve Jobs was twenty-one, and Steve Wozniak was twenty-five.
Wayne was the grown-up in the room.
He wrote their partnership contract. He even drew Apple's very first logo.

So why walk away?
Jobs had just borrowed fifteen thousand dollars to build their first big order.
If Apple went bust, the partners were personally on the hook, and Wayne was the only one with real money to lose.

So he took the eight hundred dollars.
About a year later, he took fifteen hundred more to give up any claim, for good.

Oh, and his copy of that contract? He says he sold it for five hundred bucks.
In 2011, the same papers sold at auction for one point six million.

And ten percent of Apple today? About five hundred billion dollars.
Wayne says he has no regrets.
Would you have stayed?

## Fact sources
- Wikipedia "Ronald Wayne": born 17 May 1934; Apple partnership 1 April 1976 with 45/45/10 split; drafted the agreement, drew the first logo, wrote the Apple I manual; withdrew twelve days later for $800; $1,500 about a year later to forfeit claims; personal liability and Jobs's $15,000 line of credit for the Byte Shop order; one regret was selling his copy of the contract for $500.
- Marketplace, 14 Dec 2011 ("Poor Ronald Wayne"): $800 plus $1,500, $2,300 total; KQED: left eleven days after signing, has said he has no regrets.
- KQED / Sotheby's, Dec 2011: founding contract sold for $1.59 million to Eduardo Cisneros. The seller (Wade Saadi) says he paid Wayne "several thousand dollars", hence "he says".
- Apple market value about $5 trillion, October 2026 (MacRumors, 14 Oct 2026: first close above $5T; stockanalysis.com $4.95T on 20 Oct 2026). Ten percent ≈ $500 billion. This is 10% of today's company, not what his stake would be after dilution, which is why the line says "ten percent of Apple today".
- Ages in April 1976: Jobs born 24 Feb 1955 (21), Wozniak born 11 Aug 1950 (25), Wayne 41.
