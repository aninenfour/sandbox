# What If 02 (voiced) · $100 in Apple at the 1980 IPO

**Format:** YouTube Short, 9:16, about 60 seconds. Voiceover (Kokoro bm_fable) with burned-in word captions, soft music, one small pop when an item lands. At every milestone the guy gets what his money would buy, and loses it in the crashes.
**Numbers:** real data, see `what-if-02-apple.md` (Yahoo Finance, split-adjusted, $22 IPO price, no dividends).

One sentence per line. Blank lines are short pauses.

## Script

What if you put a hundred dollars into Apple, on day one, in 1980?

Five years later, it's worth seventy-two dollars.
That's a pizza night.

By 1997, Apple is close to going broke.
Seventeen years in, you have a hundred and twenty-one dollars.
A nice dinner. That's it.

Then the dot-com boom hits.
Twelve hundred dollars! A brand new laptop.
And then the crash takes it back to two hundred and sixty.
There goes the laptop.

In 2007, the iPhone comes out, and by 2008 you've got almost seven thousand.
A used car.
Then the crash cuts it in half. Bye, car.

By 2010, ten thousand dollars. A motorbike.
In 2018, Apple hits a trillion, and you're at fifty-two thousand. A brand new car.
In 2020, you pass a hundred thousand. That's a down payment on a house.

And today? Three hundred and fifty-four thousand dollars.
From one hundred.
The hard part wasn't buying. It was holding on in 1997.
