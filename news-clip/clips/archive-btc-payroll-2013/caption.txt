In 2013, the Internet Archive offered to pay its staff in Bitcoin. Here’s what happened next 👇

🎙 Founder Brewster Kahle told the story at a Bitcoin conference in 2013. His finance chief first said no. Two weeks later he found a way, and from April 1, 2013 staff could take as much of their pay in bitcoin as they wanted. About a third of the office said yes.
🏧 Then they built a simple bitcoin-to-cash box in the office, run on trust.
