The three daily checks

08:00 Lisbon · before work

Plan the day

  • Fetch the announcements list and every live event page
  • Add new events, re-read rules on changed ones
  • Run the planner, draft the day's posts into Publer for approval

12:00 Lisbon · midday

Catch launches

  • New announcement → launch post in the next free slot
  • Event that ended this morning → mark it, schedule results post
  • Move posts if something sold out early

16:00 Lisbon · afternoon

Confirm the evening

  • Re-check closing times for tonight's last-call posts
  • Watch caps and allocations (first-come pools)
  • Hand the evening queue over, flag anything uncertain

How it runs: three scheduled tasks fire into this workspace at those times. Each one runs the checks, updates the tracker and leaves drafts in Publer. Nothing publishes without your approval unless you switch that on. Ready to schedule as soon as you say go.

Next stage: generated branded objects

What gets added

A recurring series of everyday Portuguese objects carrying the Hotcoin mark, shot like lifestyle photography, in the spirit of the branded-object posts you shared: a lime bica cup, a tram ticket, a pastel de nata box with a seal, a beach towel on the Algarve sand.

How the logo stays real

The image model generates the object with a blank label. The official logo PNG is composited onto it in code. The model never draws the logo, so it is never distorted or faked.

What it needs

An API key for an image model (fal.ai or Replicate, saved in environment settings). Roughly a cent or two per image. Each batch is approved before it is spent.
