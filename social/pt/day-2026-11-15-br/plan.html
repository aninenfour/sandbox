Meme rules

How they're made

  • Photo is serious, black and white, anonymous (back view or tiny figure). Free license only.
  • Caption is lowercase, X-screenshot style, Brazilian everyday life: churrasco, miojo, gerente do banco, 0800, Pix, fim do mês.
  • No copyrighted meme templates, no real people, no politics, no football club bashing.
  • Never a product pitch inside a meme. The account name does the branding.

Next lines in the bank

  • "eu explicando pro meu pai que 'diversificar' foi comprar 4 memecoins"
  • "o Pix de volta do meu amigo que disse que ia me pagar quando o BTC subisse"
  • "minha mãe vendo o extrato depois de eu dizer que 'cripto é igual poupança'"

梗图规则：照片严肃、黑白、匿名（背影或远景小人物），仅用免费授权图；文字小写、模仿推文截图风格，取材巴西日常（烤肉、泡面、银行经理、客服热线、Pix、月底）。不用有版权的梗图模板，不出现真实人物，不涉政治，梗图里不做产品推广。

Next stage: generated branded objects

What gets added

A recurring series of everyday Brazilian objects carrying the Hotcoin mark, shot like lifestyle photography: a lime green cafezinho cup, a havaianas-style flip-flop on Copacabana sand, a cuia de chimarrão, a coxinha box with a seal.

How the logo stays real

The image model renders the object with a blank label. The official logo PNG is placed on it in code. The model never draws the logo.

What it needs

An image API key (fal.ai or Replicate) in environment settings. About a cent or two per image, each batch approved before spending.

下一阶段（接入图像生成 API 后）：推出「品牌生活物件」系列，以巴西日常物品为主角（绿色咖啡杯、科帕卡巴纳沙滩上的人字拖、马黛茶杯、炸鸡腿 coxinha 包装盒），拍成生活方式摄影风格。模型只生成空白标签的物体，官方 Logo 由代码后期贴合，确保 Logo 不变形、不伪造。每张约 1 到 2 美分，每批生成前先审批。
