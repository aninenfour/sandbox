The substantial majority of reserves is held in **cold storage**, offline and away from the internet. Hot wallets hold only what's needed to keep day-to-day withdrawals running smoothly. Most attacks need a live connection, and most of the funds simply don't have one.

## Part 2: Do these 5 things today

> **Image:** `02-five-settings.png`

All five live in one place in your Hotcoin account: **Account Security.**

### 01 · Turn on Google Verification (2FA)
Link your email, turn on phone verification, then go one step further: **enable Google Verification.** An authenticator app generates a new code every 30 seconds on your own device. Codes sent by SMS can be intercepted through SIM-swap attacks. Codes on your device can't.

### 02 · Set your Anti-Phishing Code
This is the most underrated setting in crypto. Choose a personal code, and every genuine email from Hotcoin will display it. If an email claims to be from Hotcoin and your code isn't there, **it's fake.** Don't click anything in it. Delete it.

### 03 · Add a Fund Password
Your login password gets you into your account. A **Fund Password** is a second, separate lock on withdrawals. Even if someone gets into your account, they still can't move your funds without it. Make it different from your login password.

### 04 · Use a unique password
If you've used the same password anywhere else, assume it has leaked. Data breaches happen every week, and attackers test leaked passwords on every platform they can find. Use a long, unique password for Hotcoin, and keep it in a password manager.

### 05 · Verify before you trust
Got a link, an email, or a DM from "Hotcoin"? Before you do anything, check it on **Hotcoin Verify**, our official tool for confirming real Hotcoin channels. It takes ten seconds, which is far less time than recovering from a scam.

## Part 3: After a hack, scammers move in

> **Image:** `03-scams-follow.png`

The numbers are big. According to Chainalysis, hackers stole **$3.8B** from crypto platforms in 2022, **$1.7B** in 2023, **$2.2B** in 2024 and **$3.4B** in 2025 (January to early December). And every big hack is followed by a second wave that targets users directly.

Big security news creates the perfect cover for scams. Here are three messages that do the rounds every time:

**🚩 "URGENT: move your funds to a safe wallet."**
No legitimate exchange will ever ask you to move your crypto somewhere else to "protect" it. The urgency is the scam. The "safe wallet" belongs to the scammer.

**🚩 "Hi, I'm from Hotcoin support."**
Support doesn't slide into your DMs. If you didn't open a ticket and someone messages you first claiming to be staff, it isn't support. Real support is reached through official Hotcoin channels, which you can confirm on Hotcoin Verify.

**🚩 "Claim your compensation airdrop."**
Free money, plus a link that asks you to connect your wallet or sign a transaction, adds up to a drained wallet. Compensation, if it ever exists, is never a random link.

**The tells, in one look:** a lookalike domain (hotc0in is not hotcoin), fake urgency, a missing Anti-Phishing Code, "support" that messages you first, a request for your 2FA code, free money, and a "connect wallet" button.

**The rule:** STOP. Don't click. Don't reply. Check it on Hotcoin Verify.
