## 1/4 (image 1)
New on Hotcoin: the Futures Martingale Bot 🤖

It adds to your futures position in steps when price moves against you, lowers your average entry, and takes profit on the rebound.

What it is, how it works, when to use it 🧵👇

## 2/4 (image 2)
How does it work?

Long example on $BTC:
60,000 → initial order
58,800 → add #1
57,600 → add #2

Average entry: 58,800. Take profit can trigger before price gets back to 60,000.

(Illustration only, before fees and funding.)

## 3/4 (image 3)
When does it work best?

✅ Range-bound markets
✅ Pullbacks and rebounds (Long)
✅ Rallies and retracements (Short)

❌ Strong one-way trends
❌ Extreme volatility or thin liquidity

## 4/4 (image 4)
What are the risks?

If the trend keeps going, the bot keeps adding. Exposure grows and, with leverage, liquidation can happen.

Set your max additional orders, leverage and a stop-loss you can afford.

Full guide: https://www.hotcoin.com/en_US/support/Guide-to-Using-the-MartingaleStrategy-for-ContractTrading/
