So here's how the most famous crash in history happened, in four steps.
Step one. September third, the Dow hits a record high.
Step two. On Black Thursday, panic hits, and nearly thirteen million shares change hands.
Step three. Big bankers rush in to buy, and the market calms down, for a weekend.
Step four. Black Monday and Black Tuesday wipe out about twenty-three percent, and brokers call in their loans.
By 1932, the Dow is down eighty-nine percent.
