On this exact day in 1929, Wall Street broke.

Black Tuesday, October 29, 1929. Millions of people had bought stocks with just 10% down and borrowed money for the rest. In two days the Dow lost about 23%, brokers called in their loans, and by 1932 the market was down 89%.

What the crash still teaches:
→ Borrowed money turns a drop into a wipeout
→ A calm day in a panic is not the all-clear
→ Recoveries can take decades, not weeks
