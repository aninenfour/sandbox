Step two. He says it's being scrapped for seven thousand tons of iron, top secret.
Step three. One dealer, Andre Poisson, wants it so badly he pays a bribe too.
Step four. Lustig grabs the cash and flees, and Poisson is too embarrassed to tell police.
He came back to try again, but the police got wind, and he ran.
