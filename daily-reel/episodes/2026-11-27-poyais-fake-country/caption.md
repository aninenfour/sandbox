In 1822, a Scottish general sold London a whole country. It didn't exist.

Gregor MacGregor called himself the Cazique of Poyais. He had a guidebook, printed banknotes and a £200,000 loan in London. About 250 settlers sailed out and found only jungle and swamp. More than half died. A Paris court acquitted him, and he kept selling Poyais for years.

What the Poyais scam teaches:
→ A glossy brochure is not proof
→ If you can't check it exists, don't fund it
→ Official-looking paper can be printed by anyone
