In 1822, a Scottish general sold London a whole country.
There was just one problem.
It didn't exist.
His name was Gregor MacGregor, and he called himself the Cazique of Poyais.
So how do you sell a country that isn't there? Four steps.
Step one. Make it look official. A thick guidebook, printed banknotes, and a two hundred thousand pound loan in London.
Step two. Sell the dream. A capital city, an opera house, and rivers of gold.
Step three. Ship people out. About two hundred fifty settlers sailed over and found only jungle and swamp.
Step four. Walk away. More than half of them died. MacGregor fled to France, and a court acquitted him.
He kept selling Poyais for years.
A glossy story isn't proof. Check that it's real before you put money in.
