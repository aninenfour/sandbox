In 1907, Wall Street panicked, and one woman in a black dress lent New York City $1.1 million.

Hetty Green was born on Nov 21, 1834. She turned an inheritance into a fortune of $100 to $200 million by 1916, buying what nobody wanted and always holding cash. Wall Street called her the Witch of Wall Street.

What Hetty Green still teaches:
→ "I buy when things are low and nobody wants them"
→ Cash in calm times is what lets you act in a panic
→ Patience beats chasing the crowd
