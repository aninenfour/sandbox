In 1907, Wall Street panicked, and a woman in a black dress lent New York City over a million dollars.
Her name? Hetty Green, born a hundred ninety-two years ago this week.
Wall Street called her a witch.
So how did she become the richest woman in America? Four steps.
Step one. In 1865, she inherits millions, but most of it is locked in a trust.
Step two. She buys Civil War bonds and cheap greenbacks, then waits for them to pay off.
Step three. Her rule: "I buy when things are low and nobody wants them."
Step four. She always keeps cash, so when the 1907 panic hits, she's the one lending.
She dies in 1916, worth over a hundred million dollars.
Patience and cash let you be brave when everyone else is scared.
