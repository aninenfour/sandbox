The smartest man who ever lived lost a fortune to FOMO.
It's 1720, and Isaac Newton, the guy who figured out gravity, buys shares in the South Sea Company.
The pitch? Exclusive trade with South America, and everyone in London wants in.
Step one. The price triples, so Newton sells and walks away with a nice profit.
Step two. But the price keeps flying, and his friends are getting rich while he watches.
Step three. He can't take it, so he buys back in, near the top, with way more money.
Step four. By autumn the bubble pops, and the shares crash back to where they started.
Newton loses around twenty thousand pounds. That's millions today.
And legend says he couldn't stand to hear the words South Sea ever again.
So if a genius can't beat FOMO, have a plan before the hype hits you.
