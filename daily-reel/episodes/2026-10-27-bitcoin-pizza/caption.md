Two pizzas cost 10,000 bitcoin. That really happened.

In May 2010, Laszlo Hanyecz offered 10,000 BTC on a forum for a couple of pizzas. Four days later a 19-year-old took the deal, and bitcoin had its first famous real-world purchase, worth about $41 at the time.

What the pizza still teaches:
→ Every new money needs its first brave user
→ Value is only obvious in hindsight
→ No regrets beats endless what-ifs
