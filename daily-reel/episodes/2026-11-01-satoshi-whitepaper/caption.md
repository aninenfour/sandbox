Eighteen years ago, a stranger sent one email, then vanished.

On October 31, 2008, Satoshi Nakamoto posted a nine-page paper describing bitcoin. The first block came on January 3, 2009, Hal Finney got the first 10 coins nine days later, and by 2011 Satoshi was gone.

What the whitepaper still teaches:
→ Big ideas often start as a few pages nobody reads
→ Read the source, not just the headlines about it
→ Nobody needed to know who Satoshi was for the code to work
