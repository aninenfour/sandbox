Eighteen years ago, a stranger sent one email, then vanished.
Halloween, 2008, and banks are collapsing.
Someone called Satoshi Nakamoto emails out a nine-page paper.
So here's how one email turned into bitcoin, in four steps.
Step one. The paper describes digital cash with no bank in the middle.
Step two. On January third, 2009, Satoshi mines the first block, with a bank bailout headline hidden inside.
Step three. Nine days later, Hal Finney gets the first transaction ever, ten bitcoin.
Step four. By 2011, Satoshi writes "I've moved on to other things," and disappears.
About a million coins linked to Satoshi haven't moved in fifteen years.
Nobody has ever proved who Satoshi is.
The biggest ideas usually look small at first.
