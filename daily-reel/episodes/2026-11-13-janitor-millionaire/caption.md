A janitor died with $8 million, and nobody in his town knew.

Ronald Read pumped gas for 25 years in Brattleboro, Vermont, then worked as a janitor at J.C. Penney. He bought dividend stocks, reinvested every payout and held for decades. When he died in 2014 at 92, his estate was worth nearly $8 million, and he left $4.8 million to the local hospital and $1.2 million to the library.

What Ronald Read still teaches:
→ Time in the market does the heavy lifting
→ Reinvested dividends compound quietly
→ Spread out, so one bad pick can't sink you
