One man threw away 8,000 bitcoin, and he knows exactly where it is.

In 2009, IT worker James Howells mined 8,000 $BTC on a laptop. In 2013 the hard drive holding the keys went out with the rubbish to a Newport landfill. The council refused every dig request, and in 2025 the courts threw out his case. At today's price (Nov 11, 2026) those coins are worth close to $600 million.

What the landfill bitcoin still teaches:
→ Whoever holds the keys holds the coins
→ One copy is no copy: back up, then back up again
→ Small mistakes compound just like gains do
