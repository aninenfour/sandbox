One man threw away eight thousand bitcoin, and he knows exactly where it is.
James Howells, an IT worker in Newport, Wales.
In 2009, he mines the coins on a laptop, when they're worth almost nothing.
So how do you lose a fortune in a bin bag? Four steps.
Step one. The keys sit on an old hard drive, in a drawer.
Step two. In 2013, the wrong drive goes out with the rubbish, to the local landfill.
Step three. Bitcoin passes a thousand dollars, and he begs the council to dig. They say no.
Step four. In 2025, a judge throws out his lawsuit, and his appeal fails too.
The drive is still buried, holding close to six hundred million dollars.
A fortune is only as safe as its backup.
