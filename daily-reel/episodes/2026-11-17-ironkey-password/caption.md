One man has ten guesses to unlock a bitcoin fortune, and he's already used eight.

In 2011, programmer Stefan Thomas was paid 7,002 $BTC for a video explaining bitcoin, then worth about $2,000. He kept the keys on an IronKey drive that wipes itself after 10 wrong passwords, lost the paper with the password, and has used 8 tries. At today's price (Nov 17, 2026) those coins are worth about $450 million.

What the IronKey still teaches:
→ Self-custody means you are the bank, and the help desk
→ A password nobody can find is a lock with no key
→ Test your recovery plan before you need it
