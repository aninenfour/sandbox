In 2009, a Norwegian student spent twenty-seven dollars on something he called fake money.
Then he forgot about it.
Four years later, it bought him an apartment.
So how does twenty-seven dollars become a home? Four steps.
Step one. While writing an encryption thesis, Kristoffer Koch buys five thousand bitcoins for a hundred fifty kroner.
Step two. He calls it the worst of his gadget buys, and forgets the wallet exists.
Step three. In April 2013, bitcoin hits the news, and he thinks, didn't I have something like that?
Step four. It takes a full day to work out the password, and the wallet shows about eight hundred eighty-six thousand dollars.
He sells one fifth and buys an apartment in Oslo.
A small bet can grow, but only if you can still open the wallet.
