In 2009, a Norwegian student spent $27 on something he called "fake money", then forgot about it.

Kristoffer Koch bought 5,000 bitcoins for 150 kroner while writing a thesis on encryption. In April 2013, the news reminded him. After a full day working out the password, the wallet showed about $886,000. He sold one fifth and bought an apartment in Oslo.

What Koch's forgotten wallet teaches:
→ Small bets can matter more than you think
→ Time did the work, not trading
→ A coin you can't open is worth nothing: keep your password safe
