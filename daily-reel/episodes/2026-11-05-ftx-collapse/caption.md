Four years ago this week, a $32 billion crypto exchange died in nine days.

In November 2022, a leaked balance sheet showed FTX's sister trading firm, Alameda, was stuffed with FTX's own token. Binance said it would sell its FTT, customers pulled about $6 billion in 72 hours, and on November 11 FTX filed for bankruptcy. About $8 billion of customer money was missing, and Sam Bankman-Fried was later sentenced to 25 years.

What the FTX collapse still teaches:
→ A big brand is not the same as a safe balance sheet
→ When everyone wants out at once, withdrawals can freeze
→ Know who is really holding your money, and what they do with it
