When this crypto boss died, 115,000 customers lost their money too.

Gerald Cotten ran QuadrigaCX, Canada's biggest crypto exchange, and he alone held the passwords to its cold wallets. He died in India in December 2018, about C$250 million (US$190 million) in customer funds was missing, and investigators later found the wallets had been empty since April 2018. In 2020, Ontario's securities regulator called it a fraud.

What QuadrigaCX still teaches:
→ An exchange is only as safe as its controls, not its founder
→ "Only one person has the keys" is a red flag, not a feature
→ Proof of reserves beats promises
