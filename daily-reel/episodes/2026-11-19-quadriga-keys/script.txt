When this crypto boss died, a hundred and fifteen thousand customers lost their money too.
Gerald Cotten runs QuadrigaCX, Canada's biggest crypto exchange.
And he's the only person with the passwords to its cold wallets.
So how do a hundred ninety million dollars vanish with one man? Four steps.
Step one. In December 2018, Cotten dies at thirty on a trip to India.
Step two. Five weeks later, the exchange admits nobody else can open the wallets.
Step three. Investigators finally trace them, and they've been empty since April 2018.
Step four. In 2020, regulators call it a fraud: fake accounts, risky trading, and a lavish life on customer money.
Some users even asked police to dig up his body to prove he's dead.
If only one person holds the keys, nobody's money is safe.
