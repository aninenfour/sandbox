# Hotcoin LinkedIn: content strategy

Owner brief (Nov 25, 2026): grow followers on the Hotcoin LinkedIn company page, not just post. Data with our own angle,
geo and industry insight, job and career content, why trade on Hotcoin, why we run the events we run, the trading bot
(how many bots trade vs people), human stories and milestones, how-to and why-to education. Cadence asked: 2 posts a
day, plus 1 event post a day, plus 3 videos a week (Mon, Wed, Fri: how-to strategy/tool videos in a clean chart style,
and the archive/history clips we already post on IG and TikTok). Publish through Publer, scheduled directly (no draft
review step, owner Nov 25).

Owner decisions (Nov 25): bot numbers from public data only (no internal stats); archive videos must end on a finished
thought (the Kahle payroll reel was cut before the payoff).

Owner decisions (Nov 26, these override older rules here and in CLAUDE.md where they conflict):
- MAIN GOAL: more followers. Every format, time and theme is judged by followers gained and reach.
- Faces are allowed: staff, booth and team photos, real people in stories and archive clips. Logos are allowed in
  context (partners, coins, events, news). Do not drop a strong content style for caution. We still never invent
  quotes, endorsements or numbers: every claim stays true and sourced.
- Mix photo styles (real event photos, TOKEN2049, photo covers) with the grain covers so the feed is never one look.
- Hiring is a pillar: scan moledao.io/companies/hotcoin/jobs/ weekly, post open roles as a designed carousel with
  pay ranges, the Moledao link, a "DM us" call to action and many hashtags (up to ~15).
- Run day by day: the daily run produces and schedules that day's LinkedIn posts and fills the tracker
  (https://claude.ai/artifact/R7pmTZfEGjH8Kp1DyLf4x5). Themes and rules are reviewed and refreshed at least weekly
  (Monday), so nothing repeats. See linkedin/RUNBOOK.md.

## 1. Where we start (Publer, Aug 27 to Nov 25, 2026)
- 100 LinkedIn posts in 90 days, most mirrored from X. Best post: 9 engagements. Many posts: 0.
- What worked: #Hotcoin101 education (position sizing 9, candles 8, blockchain 7), the Bitcoin whitepaper anniversary (4).
- What died: referral promo, memecoin "every era one app", TradFi earnings with a link, TOKEN2049 countdown (0 to 2).
- Publer best times for this page: Mon 23:00, Tue/Wed 13:00, Tue/Wed/Thu 22:00 to 23:00 (workspace time; logs use
  Beijing time, so 13:00 = Asia lunch, 22:30 = Europe afternoon / US morning).
- Lesson: copy-pasted X posts do not work here. LinkedIn wants posts that teach, prove or show data, written for LinkedIn.

## 2. What the research says (sources at the bottom)
| Finding | What we do |
|---|---|
| Company page posts reach about 1.6% of followers; personal profiles get far more reach (van der Blom 2025) | Every post must earn reshares and saves; ask staff to reshare the weekly flagship from their own accounts |
| Native documents (PDF carousels) have the top engagement rate, 7.0%; multi-image 6.45%; video 6.0%; image 5.3%; text 4.5%; poll 4.2%; link 3.25% (Socialinsider, 2025 data, 26k pages) | Data and 101 posts go out as PDF carousels; no bare link posts |
| A link in the post body cuts reach by about 19% to 36% (van der Blom Apr 2025, LinkPost 2026) | No links in the body, except the event post that needs a sign-up link |
| Dwell time, saves and comment quality drive ranking; topic relevance beats hashtags (360Brew coverage, 2025 to 2026) | Swipeable carousels, one topic per post, 3 hashtags max |
| Buffer (2M posts, Aug 2025): more posts per week means more reach per post, no frequency penalty. van der Blom (2026): daily posting gives more total reach but about 26% less per post | 2 posts a day on weekdays is fine if spaced 8+ hours; weekends lighter |
| Video on LinkedIn: views peak at 2 to 3 min for pages (Socialinsider 2026); vertical under 60 s does well in the feed (van der Blom). You cannot post straight to the vertical video feed; LinkedIn places videos there by relevance | Archive clips 45 to 75 s; how-to videos 60 to 120 s; 4:5 (works on desktop and mobile), burned-in subtitles |
| Pages that post weekly get 2x engagement; 3 to 5 hashtags; organic posts can be targeted by region, industry, seniority at no cost (LinkedIn) | Use free audience targeting on geo posts (e.g. a Vietnam data post targeted to Vietnam) |
| Employees have 10x more connections than a page has followers; advocacy raised page followers 2.5x (LinkedIn internal data) | Use "Notify employees" once a week on the flagship post; staff reshare without showing their face |
| A Page newsletter invites all followers by notification on the first edition; each edition goes to subscribers by email and in-app | Launch a monthly Page newsletter, "Hotcoin Data Desk" (biggest follower lever we have that costs nothing) |

## 6. Post rules (LinkedIn)
- Hook in the first two lines (about 200 characters show before "see more"). Lead with the number or the tension.
- 600 to 1,300 characters for data and 101 posts; short lines; arrows (→) for lists, no em dashes.
- End with a specific question (two-option picks get more comments than open ones on our IG; test the same here).
- 3 hashtags max, topical (#Bitcoin #CryptoMarkets #Hotcoin101). No tag spam.
- Links only in event posts. Data posts: "Full data in the carousel" instead of a link.
- Date of the data on the slide; sources in the caption, not as tiny footer text on the graphic.
- Futures, Earn and price posts carry "Not financial advice" or "Futures trading is high risk" in the caption.
- Never name competitors. No staff faces or names. No real people implying endorsement (archive clips are news, credited).
- Reply to every comment within the first hour; comment from the page on 3 to 5 industry posts a day (the page's
  own comments raise its visibility).
- Carousels: 1080x1350 PNG pages merged to PDF, 5 to 10 pages, cover page = the hook, last page = "Follow Hotcoin for
  weekly data".

## 7. Video rules
- 4:5 (1080x1350) for LinkedIn; 9:16 originals stay for IG/TikTok.
- Burned-in subtitles; frame 1 shows the full design; no blank opening.
- Archive clips must end on a finished thought: read the transcript past the planned out-point, end on the payoff
  sentence, and check the last 0.2 s of the render (no half word or next sentence starting). See news-clip/RUNBOOK.md.
- How-to videos: one idea, chart-led, numbers checked, risk line in the caption. Original music from
  hotcoin-tradfi/scripts/make-*-beat.py.

## 7c. How-to videos (Nov 27, after owner feedback on v1 and v2)
- v1 (plain chart) was "0 value"; v2 (everything as halftone pixels) was unreadable, slow, empty at the bottom.
- Rules now: mix styles inside one video (duotone photo cutouts that act out the line, clean candle/line charts,

## 8. Bots vs people: how we use public data
No reliable public figure separates bot and human volume on centralized exchanges (Tradersunion, Birdeye methodology
notes). The "65% to 80% of crypto volume is bots" numbers that circulate are loosely sourced. So:
- Use only figures with a named source and a clear scope (e.g. DEX/on-chain bot share, a published exchange report),
  and say what the number measures (trade count vs volume, chain, period).
- Frame posts around the question ("How much of crypto is traded by machines? Here's what the data can and can't tell
  us") and around how our bot / copy trading works with a worked example.
- If the team later shares internal numbers, they become the flagship Data Desk post.
