## 2. Background art (FLUX schnell, about $0.003 per image)

```

like rising steps of a leaderboard, floating in a black void on the right half of the frame. Pale lime backlight (#B8F26A) glows

```

soft lime light glowing out and a few glossy lime coins with blank faces floating up. On a glossy black floor in the right half,
at the vertical middle. Dark studio, haze, lime rim light. The left half and the top third are empty and black.

Regional (Brazil example): keep the brand palette, add one local cue (Copacabana wave pavement, palms) and one warm accent.
Never flags (their text breaks) or landmarks with disputed image rights.
