## Quick notes

**Upload order.** Post the Short first, then the 16:9 an hour or two later. The Short feeds discovery, the long cut catches search.

**Titles.** Every 16:9 title is under 60 characters so nothing truncates in search. Shorts titles are under 40.

**Hashtags.** Three per description, placed at the end. YouTube shows the first three above the title. More than three and it shows none, so do not add extras.

**Chapters.** Skipped deliberately. These run around a minute and YouTube needs at least three chapters of ten seconds each, which would fragment them for no benefit.

**Thumbnails.** Same rule as the videos: paper, ink, one green element, no faces and no arrows. Consistency across the row in the channel grid is worth more than any single thumbnail.
