# FILE 007 · The First Bubble Had Tulips In It

**16:9 title**

```
Tulip Mania Explained: The First Bubble, and Every One Since
```

**Shorts title**

```
One Tulip Bulb Cost More Than a House
```
(37 characters)

**Description**

```
In 1637 a single flower bulb sold for more than a house. Not a metaphor. An actual bulb, in Haarlem, for more than a canal-side property.

Tulips arrived in the Netherlands from the Ottoman empire and turned into a status symbol. The prized ones were streaked with colour, which nobody could explain at the time. Then people stopped trading flowers altogether. Bulbs sit underground most of the year, so traders started buying paper promises on next season's crop, and the whole market moved out of the gardens and into the taverns.

Prices went vertical. Then an auction found no buyers, and it was over in weeks.

In this episode:
→ How a flower became a luxury asset
→ Why forward contracts turned a slow market into a fast one
→ What the peak actually looked like in wages
→ The three things every mania since has borrowed
→ 1637 lined up against the modern version

Bubbles are never really about the asset. They're about the story that makes the price sound reasonable.
```

# FILE 008 · FUD, FOMO and the Two Moods of the Market

**16:9 title**

```
FUD and FOMO Explained: The Two Moods Every Market Has
```

**Shorts title**

```
FUD vs FOMO, Explained in 60 Seconds
```
(36 characters)

**Description**

```
Two words cover most of what gets said about markets. FUD and FOMO.

FUD is fear, uncertainty and doubt. Negative talk that gets around a lot faster than whatever it's actually about. Sometimes that's someone talking their book. Just as often it's a real risk that turned up early and nobody wanted to hear it yet.

FOMO runs the other way. Buying because something's already moving, rather than because you'd decided you wanted to own it. It never feels like FOMO while it's happening. It feels like finally being sure.

One tends to show up early. The other shows up late. This is the sixty second version of both.

In this episode:
→ What FUD actually means, and when it's manipulation rather than information
→ Why fear moves quicker than the facts that would settle it
→ What FOMO is, and why greed arrives after the move instead of before it
→ Three questions that work on either mood
→ What a wave of FUD, sudden FOMO and total silence usually mean underneath

Neither one is a signal. They're the weather around it.

#crypto #trading #FOMO
```
