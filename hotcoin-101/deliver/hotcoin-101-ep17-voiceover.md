# FILE 017 · Bonds, or Lending to a Government

```
The most boring thing in finance is lending money to a government, and somehow it sets the price of everything else, crypto included.

A bond is just a loan. You hand over a thousand dollars, they pay you, say, forty a year, that's the coupon, and you get the thousand back at the end.

Here's the weird bit though. If new bonds start paying fifty, nobody wants your forty, so to sell it you have to cut the price. Rates up, old bond prices down, it's just maths.

And the yield on the US ten-year is the number everyone watches, because it's what you earn for taking basically no risk.

So in 2022, when it climbed from about one and a half percent to almost four, uhm, everything risky had to get cheaper to compete, and bitcoin lost about two thirds.

Safe money sets the bar every other bet has to clear.
```
