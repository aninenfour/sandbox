# FILE 015 · Whale, Shrimp, Bagholder

```
Crypto has a whole zoo, whales, shrimp, bagholders, and, well, you're probably in it somewhere.

A whale is a wallet big enough to move the price on its own. Trackers usually draw the line at a thousand bitcoin, so when one sells, everyone else feels the wave.

A shrimp is the opposite, under one bitcoin, and honestly, that's most of us.

And the bagholder isn't about size at all. It's whoever bought near the top and is still holding, uhm, waiting for it to come back.

Here's the catch though, an address isn't a person, because one exchange wallet can hold coins for a huge crowd of users.

But when a handful of wallets really do hold most of a coin, one of them selling can sink everyone else, so before you buy, check who holds the rest.

Concentration is a risk, even when the whale seems friendly.
```
