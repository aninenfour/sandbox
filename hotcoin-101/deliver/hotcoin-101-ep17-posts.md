## LinkedIn

```
Government bonds are the least exciting asset most people will ever hear about, and one of the most important for anyone holding risk assets, crypto included 🏛️

A bond is a loan with a schedule. You lend a fixed amount, receive a fixed coupon each year, and get the principal back at maturity. Because the coupon is fixed, the bond's market price has to adjust when rates change. If new bonds are issued paying more, an existing bond paying less is only attractive at a lower price. That is why bond prices and yields move in opposite directions, mechanically.

The US 10-year Treasury yield matters beyond the bond market because it is widely used as the benchmark "risk-free" return. Every other investment, from equities to property to crypto, is implicitly compared against it: if a near-riskless asset pays more, riskier assets need a lower price, or a higher expected return, to stay competitive.

2022 is the clearest recent example. The 10-year yield rose from 1.51% at the end of 2021 to 3.88% at the end of 2022. Over the same year, bitcoin fell about 64% (Coin Metrics reference rate, 31 December to 31 December). Many forces were at work, but the higher hurdle set by safe money was one of them.
```
