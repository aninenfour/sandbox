# FILE 021 · Ape, Rug, Degen

Facts used on screen and below (checked):
- Aping in: buying fast and big with little or no research (Ledger, Crypto.com glossaries).
- Squid Game token ($SQUID), late Oct to 1 Nov 2021: peaked around $2,861, holders could not sell, then it collapsed to near zero within minutes; about $3.38M was drained (Gizmodo, Cointelegraph, PC Gamer).
- Chainalysis 2022 Crypto Crime Report: rug pulls were 37% of all crypto scam revenue in 2021, more than $2.8B, up from 1% in 2020. (Most of that total was the Thodex exchange collapse.)
- $LIBRA, February 2025: promoted in a post by Argentina's president Javier Milei, reached roughly a $4.5B valuation, fell more than 90% within hours; insider wallets cashed out about $87M to $107M (Bubblemaps, Lookonchain); 86% of traders lost money, $251M in total (Nansen). Milei deleted the post and denied involvement.
- Degen: short for degenerate gambler, often used about oneself.
- The chart shapes on screen are labelled illustrative.

Crypto slang is funny. A lot of it is also a risk disclosure in disguise.

"Aping in" means buying quickly and in size without research. A "rug pull" is what that behaviour invites: a team launches a token, attracts buyers, then removes the liquidity or sells its own allocation, and the market for the token disappears. "Degen", short for degenerate gambler, is used by traders about themselves, often with pride.

The numbers behind the jokes are serious. Chainalysis attributed 37% of all crypto scam revenue in 2021, more than $2.8 billion, to rug pulls, up from 1% the year before. The Squid Game token of November 2021 traded above $2,800 before collapsing to near zero within minutes, with holders unable to sell. In February 2025, the $LIBRA memecoin reached a valuation of roughly $4.5 billion after a post by Argentina's president, then fell more than 90% within hours as insider wallets withdrew an estimated $87M to $107M.

The practical question for any new token is structural: who controls the liquidity, whether it is locked, and who holds the supply.
