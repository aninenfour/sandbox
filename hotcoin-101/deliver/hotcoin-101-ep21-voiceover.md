# FILE 021 · Ape, Rug, Degen

```
Crypto has a whole dictionary of jokes. Ape, rug, degen. And the funny thing is, well, most of them are warnings wearing a costume.

Ape first. Aping in means buying fast, buying big, without reading a thing, just because everyone else is jumping.

Which brings us to the rug. A team launches a token, everyone apes in, and then the team pulls the liquidity, or dumps their bags, and the floor just disappears.

In twenty twenty-one, a token named after Squid Game shot past two thousand dollars, and buyers couldn't even sell. Then it dropped to almost zero in minutes, and its creators were gone with about three million. That same year, Chainalysis counted rug pulls as thirty-seven percent of all crypto scam revenue, more than two point eight billion dollars.

And it's not ancient history. In February twenty twenty-five, a token called LIBRA got a post from Argentina's president, hit a four billion dollar valuation, and crashed more than ninety percent within hours, as insider wallets cashed out around a hundred million.

Then there's degen, short for degenerate, as in degenerate gambler. And here's the twist, people say it about themselves, proudly.

When a community jokes about a risk all the time, the risk is real. So before you ape in, ask one question. Who can pull the rug?
```
