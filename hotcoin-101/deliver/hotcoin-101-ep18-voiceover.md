# FILE 018 · Risk of Ruin: The Maths of Blowing Up

```
Lose half your money and, well, you'd think you need half back. You don't, you need to double it.

That's the maths of a drawdown. Lose ten percent, you need eleven to get back. Lose half, you need a hundred. Lose seventy, and you need two hundred and thirty-three percent, which, uhm, mostly doesn't happen.

And losses don't arrive politely, one at a time. Flip a fair coin a hundred times and a run of five losses in a row is more likely than not.

So here's the whole game. Risk two percent a trade, and five losses leaves you with ninety percent, annoying but fine. Risk twenty percent, and the same streak leaves you with a third, needing to triple just to break even.

Same trader, same luck, totally different ending.

Staying in the game isn't the boring part of trading. It's the strategy.
```
