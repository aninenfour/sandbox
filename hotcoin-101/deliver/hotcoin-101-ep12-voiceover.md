# FILE 012 · Leverage Is a Clock, Not a Multiplier

```
Leverage doesn't make you right faster, it makes being early fatal.

Put in a hundred at twenty times and you're moving two thousand, so a five percent move against you is all of it.

And every step up pulls your exit closer, half the price away at two times, and barely five percent at twenty.

Same idea, same market, and ten times rides out the dip and ends up right, while twenty times gets closed out halfway.

So leverage isn't a multiplier, it's a clock, it decides how long you're allowed to be wrong.

And the clock isn't free either, funding is charged on the whole position and comes out of your margin.

So before you add a zero, know your exit, know the cost, and know how long your idea needs.

Size it for how long you might be wrong, not for how sure you feel.
```
