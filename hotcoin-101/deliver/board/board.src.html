What it is它是什么

A narrator, not a sticker是讲解员，不只是贴纸

The logo就是 Logo

Same green, same circle, same size as the dot in the lockup. Every appearance is brand exposure, and every video ends with it turning back into the mark.与标志中的圆点同色、同形、同比例。每次出镜都是品牌曝光，每集结尾它都会变回标志本身。

One eye一只眼睛

No mouth, no text, no voice of its own. Emotion comes from lids, brow and pupil, so it reads the same in every market and every language.没有嘴、没有文字、没有自己的声音。情绪全靠眼睑、眉毛和瞳孔表达，在任何市场、任何语言中都一样好懂。

A guide视线向导

It looks where the viewer should look, travels to the next idea before the voice gets there, and rides along with what we explain. Viewers follow it without thinking.它看向观众该看的地方，在旁白之前先移动到下一个重点，并跟着讲解内容一起"出演"。观众会不自觉地跟着它走。

What it can do它能做什么

Moves like a character像真正的角色一样动

Every move is cued to a word in the voiceover, so the dot always arrives as the idea does.每个动作都卡在旁白的某个词上，圆点总是和观点同时到达。

glide

Travels to the next idea with a small wind-up and a soft overshoot.带一点预备动作滑向下一个重点，到达时轻微回弹。

hop

Jumps in an arc and squashes on landing. Used for timelines and stairs.弧线跳跃，落地时被压扁。用于时间线、台阶。

drop

Falls in from above with a bigger squash. The cold open entrance.从上方掉落，压扁更明显。开场入场方式。

ride

Follows a chart line or sits inside an object: a bus seat, a crate, a tree.沿着走势线移动，或待在物体里：公交座位、箱子、树上。

size

Grows and shrinks on cue. It swelled like a bubble and popped in FILE 020.可以按节奏变大变小。第 020 集里它像泡沫一样膨胀然后爆掉。

full stop

Lands as the dot at the end of the episode title.落在每集标题末尾，充当句号。

alive

Blinks, breathes, glances around between moves. Never frozen.会眨眼、呼吸、在动作之间四处张望，从不僵住。

wipe

Page changes open as a circle out of the dot's position.换页时，画面以圆点为中心圆形展开。

How we use it使用规范

Rules that keep it ours保持品牌一致的规则

  • Always brand green #7EC25A, always a perfect circle.始终使用品牌绿 #7EC25A，始终是正圆。
  • Every video ends with it landing on the logo and going plain.每支视频都以它落入 Logo、变回纯色圆点收尾。
  • Thumbnails: very large, with one strong mood.封面中要足够大，配一种强烈情绪。
  • Its gags act out the lesson: the joke and the point are the same thing.桥段必须演绎知识点：笑点就是重点。
  • Never on a green background. It disappears.不要放在绿色背景上，会"隐身"。
  • In videos: no mouth, arms or text. The eye does the acting. Meme stickers may break this rule; that is the joke.视频中不加嘴巴、手臂或文字，表演全靠眼睛。表情包可以打破这条规则，反差就是笑点。
  • Don't recolour it for campaigns. Props around it can change; the dot can't.活动中不要给它换颜色。周边道具可以变，圆点不能变。
  • Red stays for "down, wrong, careful", never on the dot itself.红色只表示"下跌、错误、注意"，绝不用在圆点本身。

The current alternative现有的另一种方案

Why not the full logo?为什么不用完整 Logo？

Our Chinese social channels already turn the whole logo into a character: the white stroke with the green dot, plus arms and legs. It is recognisable, but it has never settled into one look, and it is hard to make as a real object.我们的中文社媒已经把整个 Logo 做成了角色：白色笔画加绿色圆点，再加上手脚。它辨识度高，但始终没有固定成统一的形象，而且很难做成实物。

Hotcoin Chinese social media, three posts, three different charactersHotcoin 中文社媒：三条内容，三个不同的角色

Full-logo character完整 Logo 角色

  • Not settled: the face, the mouth and the dot's shape change from post to post.形象不统一：五官、嘴巴和圆点的形状每次都不一样。
  • Heavy, angled form with the dot hanging off one side. As a toy it is top-heavy and needs a stand.造型笨重且倾斜，圆点挂在一侧。做成玩具头重脚轻，需要支架。
  • Two parts, two colours, thin limbs and many seams: costly moulds and weak joints in mass production.两个部件、两种颜色、细长四肢、接缝多：量产时模具成本高、连接处容易坏。
  • At emoji size the white stroke turns into a blob.缩小到表情尺寸时，白色笔画会糊成一团。

The dot小绿点

  • One look everywhere: the same sphere and eye in videos, stickers and merch.处处一致：视频、贴纸和周边中是同一个球体、同一只眼睛。
  • A ball stands on its own, rolls, squeezes and survives a bag.球体自己就能立住，能滚、能捏，放包里也不怕压。
  • One shape, one colour, one eye: the cheapest possible mould, from keychain to 3-metre inflatable.一个形状、一种颜色、一只眼睛：从钥匙扣到 3 米充气模型，模具成本最低。
  • Reads at 24 pixels, and still turns back into the logo at the end of every video.24 像素也能看清，而且每支视频结尾都会变回 Logo。
