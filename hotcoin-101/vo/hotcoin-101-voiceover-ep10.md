---

**00:36 – 00:41.5 · The one people get wrong**

> The one people get wrong is the stop, it's a trigger, not a promise.

Direction: no pause before "it's a trigger". Comma, not full stop.

---

**00:41.5 – 00:49.5 · Speed, price, certainty. Pick two**
*On screen: three switches, only ever two on at once*

> And you only ever get two of the three, speed, price, certainty, whichever pair you pick is the order you just placed.

Direction: read the three words evenly, like items on a shelf, then carry straight into the rest without a pause.

---

**00:49.5 – 00:56.5 · When each one hurts**

---

**00:56.5 – 01:02 · The takeaway**

Direction: land this. Small pause before "it's part of the trade".
