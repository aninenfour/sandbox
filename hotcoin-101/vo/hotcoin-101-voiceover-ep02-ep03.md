### Paste block, FILE 003

```
Everyone reads candles all day. Not everyone knows what's in one.

A candle is really just four numbers. The open, where price started. The close, where it ended. And the high and low in between.

Green means it closed above the open. Red means it closed below. That's all the colour is telling you.

That thin line is the wick. It shows where price got to, before it was pushed back down.

The same hour looks different depending on your chart. Twelve candles on the five minute. One candle on the hourly.

The shape does tell you something. A big body means one side had control. A small one means neither did.

But it won't tell you why price moved, or how much actually traded. For that you need volume.

And it's an old idea. Japanese rice traders were drawing these in the 1700s.

So always check the timeframe before you read the shape.
```
