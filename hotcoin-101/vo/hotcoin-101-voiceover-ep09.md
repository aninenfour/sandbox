### Paste block, FILE 009

```
Two ways to hold crypto, and both of them cost you something.

The strange part is you never really hold a coin, there isn't one, it's just a line in a ledger.

So one option is you keep the key yourself, and nobody can freeze it, and nobody can help you.

Or somebody keeps it for you, and what you're holding then is a claim rather than a key.

And that's the whole trade, honestly. Your own mistakes on one side, somebody else's on the other.

About a fifth of all bitcoin is sitting in wallets nobody can open.

Which is why self custody tends to fail quietly, a lost phrase, a dead drive, while custody fails loudly.

And so most people split it, trading size where they trade, the rest somewhere slower.

Because custody isn't a safety setting, it's a choice about who you'd rather trust.
```
